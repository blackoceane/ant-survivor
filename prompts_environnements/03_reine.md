# 3. Chambre de la reine — niveau 3 (final)

La salle royale au fond de la fourmilière, envahie par le parasite : champignons violets,
veines vertes lumineuses, spores qui flottent, un grand lit royal en pétales au centre.
Ambiance sombre, étrange et triste, mais encore belle (la reine peut être guérie).

---

## Prompt A — SOL (tuiles raccordables)

```
STYLE (keep identical for every image): high-quality top-down 3/4 view pixel art for a 2D adventure game, like classic Zelda or Stardew Valley camera angle. Miniature world seen at the scale of an ant: everyday garden things are gigantic. Cinematic, warm and magical mood inspired by macro nature photography and whimsical miniature-world fantasy films: rich saturated greens and earthy browns, soft golden rim light, light always coming from the top-left, gentle soft shadows falling to the bottom-right, clean dark outlines on objects, limited cohesive palette, crisp pixels, no blur, no anti-aliasing smear, no photorealism.

LEVEL MOOD: the queen's royal chamber deep underground, invaded by a parasitic fungus: dark purples and plums, sickly glowing green veins, floating spores, eerie but beautiful, low light with glowing accents.

CONTENT: a tileset sheet of ground textures for the queen's chamber. Square image 1024x1024 pixels divided into an invisible 4x4 grid of perfectly square tiles (each tile 256x256 pixels), tiles touching edge to edge with no gaps. Every tile must be SEAMLESS and TILEABLE on all four sides, flat top-down texture only.
Row 1: four variations of dark purple-brown royal chamber floor (polished old earth, faint hexagonal honeycomb pattern).
Row 2: four variations of floor covered by fungal mycelium carpet (grey-green threads with tiny glowing green dots).
Row 3: four variations of the TOP of infected earth walls seen from above (dark soil with small purple mushroom caps and green veins), to be used as solid wall tiles.
Row 4: floor with glowing green veins, floor with a puddle of glowing green ooze, floor with scattered purple spores, healed floor (warm golden-brown, clean, a few pink petals) for the happy ending.
Consistent lighting and palette across all 16 tiles.

STRICTLY AVOID: any text, letters, numbers, labels, titles, watermarks, signatures, UI, frames, borders, grid lines drawn on the image, characters, people, animals, insects, perspective distortion, isometric diamond tiles, side view, blurry or painterly areas.
```
