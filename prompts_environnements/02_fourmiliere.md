# 2. Fourmilière — niveau 2

Sous terre : tunnels de terre tassée creusés par les fourmis, racines qui traversent les murs,
champignons et vers luisants qui éclairent, salles de stockage de graines et nurserie d'œufs.
Ambiance chaude et ambrée, un peu mystérieuse. Quelques spores violettes : l'infection commence.

---

## Prompt A — SOL (tuiles raccordables)

```
STYLE (keep identical for every image): high-quality top-down 3/4 view pixel art for a 2D adventure game, like classic Zelda or Stardew Valley camera angle. Miniature world seen at the scale of an ant: everyday garden things are gigantic. Cinematic, warm and magical mood inspired by macro nature photography and whimsical miniature-world fantasy films: rich saturated greens and earthy browns, soft golden rim light, light always coming from the top-left, gentle soft shadows falling to the bottom-right, clean dark outlines on objects, limited cohesive palette, crisp pixels, no blur, no anti-aliasing smear, no photorealism.

LEVEL MOOD: underground ant colony, warm amber light from glowing mushrooms, cozy but mysterious.

CONTENT: a tileset sheet of ground textures for the anthill tunnels. Square image 1024x1024 pixels divided into an invisible 4x4 grid of perfectly square tiles (each tile 256x256 pixels), tiles touching edge to edge with no gaps. Every tile must be SEAMLESS and TILEABLE on all four sides, flat top-down texture only.
Row 1: four variations of packed light-brown tunnel floor (smooth trampled earth, tiny ant footprints, small pebbles).
Row 2: four variations of darker chamber floor (darker soil, scattered seed husks, small roots).
Row 3: four variations of the TOP of the earth walls seen from above (dark rough soil with thin roots and embedded stones), to be used as solid wall tiles.
Row 4: floor with a faint golden pheromone trail of dots, floor with a few purple fungal spores (infection beginning), floor with crumbs of stored food, damp floor with a small water seep.
Consistent lighting and palette across all 16 tiles.

STRICTLY AVOID: any text, letters, numbers, labels, titles, watermarks, signatures, UI, frames, borders, grid lines drawn on the image, characters, people, animals, insects, perspective distortion, isometric diamond tiles, side view, blurry or painterly areas.
```
