# 1. Jardin géant (extérieur) — niveau 1

L'enfant, devenu minuscule, arrive dans le jardin des grands-parents : herbe haute comme une forêt,
gouttes de rosée, cailloux grands comme des maisons. Un petit indice discret : une flaque verdâtre de pesticide.

---

## Prompt A — SOL (tuiles raccordables)

```
STYLE (keep identical for every image): high-quality top-down 3/4 view pixel art for a 2D adventure game, like classic Zelda or Stardew Valley camera angle. Miniature world seen at the scale of an ant: everyday garden things are gigantic. Cinematic, warm and magical mood inspired by macro nature photography and whimsical miniature-world fantasy films: rich saturated greens and earthy browns, soft golden rim light, light always coming from the top-left, gentle soft shadows falling to the bottom-right, clean dark outlines on objects, limited cohesive palette, crisp pixels, no blur, no anti-aliasing smear, no photorealism.

CONTENT: a tileset sheet of ground textures for the giant garden level. Square image 1024x1024 pixels divided into an invisible 4x4 grid of perfectly square tiles (each tile 256x256 pixels), tiles touching edge to edge with no gaps. Every tile must be SEAMLESS and TILEABLE on all four sides, flat top-down texture only, no objects standing on it.
Row 1: four variations of short lush grass seen from above (soft green, tiny clover leaves, small grass tufts, a few dew sparkles).
Row 2: four variations of warm brown garden soil path (packed earth, tiny pebbles like boulders, small cracks).
Row 3: four transition tiles mixing grass and soil (grass patches spreading over the soil, natural irregular edges).
Row 4: moss carpet, damp dark soil with fallen petals, gravel of giant sand grains, shallow puddle edge with water.
Consistent lighting and palette across all 16 tiles.

STRICTLY AVOID: any text, letters, numbers, labels, titles, watermarks, signatures, UI, frames, borders, grid lines drawn on the image, characters, people, animals, insects, perspective distortion, isometric diamond tiles, side view, blurry or painterly areas.
```
